# SIH 2025 Problem Statement Themes and Examples

## Themes / categories

The SIH 2025 catalogue grouped problem statements into themes such as:

- Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
- MedTech / BioTech / HealthTech
- Smart Education
- Disaster Management
- Smart Automation
- Transportation & Logistics
- Clean & Green Technology
- Blockchain & Cybersecurity
- Heritage & Culture
- Renewable / Sustainable Energy
- Fitness & Sports
- Robotics and Drones
- Travel & Tourism
- Miscellaneous

Each PS is also tagged as either **Software** or **Hardware**.

## Category distribution

Based on a public scraping exercise of the 2025 problem statement list:

- Software: ~66% (about 90 problem statements)
- Hardware: ~33% (about 45 problem statements)
- Total: ~135 problem statements

This can change every year; use the official portal for the exact number.

## How to use the PS list

1. Download the official Excel/PDF from sih.gov.in or use the portal's searchable table.
2. Filter by Software/Hardware based on your team's strength.
3. Filter by theme that matches your interest.
4. Read the description, background, expected solution and dataset availability.
5. Shortlist 5–10 PSs and rate them using the selection criteria in `03_team_and_ps_selection.md`.
