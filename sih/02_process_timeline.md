# SIH Process Flow and Timeline

## Overall process

1. Problem statements are submitted by ministries, state departments, PSUs, industries and NGOs on the SIH portal.
2. Problem statements are reviewed and finalized.
3. College SPOCs register and "block" problem statements the college wants to work on.
4. The college conducts an **internal hackathon**.
5. College SPOC nominates top teams (e.g., 30 shortlist + 5 waitlist).
6. Nominated team leaders register on the SIH portal and submit the idea/PPT.
7. National shortlisting happens.
8. Shortlisted teams attend the **Grand Finale** at an assigned nodal centre.
9. Final judging and winners are announced.

## Internal hackathon (most important filter)

This is where most teams are eliminated. Treat it as seriously as the national finale.

Typical internal-hackathon rounds:

1. **Preliminary / PPT round** — teams submit a PPT/abstract. Judges shortlist based on the idea.
2. **Prototype / offline round** — shortlisted teams present a working or partial prototype.
3. **Final selection** — top teams are chosen to be nominated for the national portal.

A college may run a 12-hour or full-day internal hackathon and select the top 15, 30 or 50 teams, depending on its size.

## Portal idea submission

- Only the team leader can submit the idea on the SIH portal in many editions.
- The idea submission is usually a short PDF/PPT (official template, max 6 slides).
- Required sections: problem statement ID/title/theme/category, proposed solution, technical approach, feasibility, impact/benefits, research/references.
- Submitting a public GitHub link and a short PPT is common.

## Grand finale

- Held offline at nodal centres across India.
- Teams travel to the assigned centre.
- Duration: approximately 36 hours for software; hardware may run for more days.
- Multiple evaluation rounds, with mentors/judges reviewing progress.
- Final round is an end-to-end demo/presentation.

## Timeline (tentative, 2024/2025 reference)

| Stage | Approximate time (2025 as reference) |
|-------|--------------------------------------|
| Problem statements released | August (e.g., SIH 2024: 8 Aug 2024) |
| SPOC registration | First/second week of August |
| Internal hackathon | 10–20 September |
| Last date for nomination and idea submission | 30 September (2025); 12 September (2024) |
| National shortlisting | Late September / Early October |
| Grand finale | 3rd/4th week of November |

> Dates vary by edition. Follow the official [sih.gov.in](https://sih.gov.in) notice and your college's SPOC.

## Nodal centres

The grand finale is conducted at colleges/institutes assigned as nodal centres. A few examples from 2023/2024:

- Mumbai
- LNCT Bhopal
- O P Jindal University, Raigarh
- New Horizon College of Engineering, Bangalore
- Vijayawada, Andhra Pradesh
- IIT Kharagpur

Your assigned nodal centre may be anywhere in India and will be communicated by SIH after shortlisting.

## Travel and logistics

- Colleges usually issue a stamped photo ID and consent/permission letter.
- Nodal centres arrange accommodation and food during the grand finale.
- Sleeper class rail fare may be reimbursed for some teams; confirm the exact rules for your edition.
- Mentors may travel but are not always eligible for travel reimbursement.
