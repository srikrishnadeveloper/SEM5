### ByeWorld — SIH 2022 Software winner

- Won first prize at the SIH 2022 Grand Finale.
- Problem statement: AK1088, issued by NDEAR / Department of School Education and Literacy (DoSEL).
- Prize: INR 1 lakh.
- Team leader: Vignesh Balaji Saravanarajan.
- Team members: Sumon Kumar, Supraja Sridhar, Thejas Narasimhan, Shruthi Dhanvanth, Shri Keshavinee R.

## What these examples show

- Teams from SSN have won SIH in both 2022 and 2023, so it is absolutely possible.
- Winners combine strong tech execution, cloud/ML skills, good presentation and faculty/mentor support.
- Even finalist-level participation is a major learning experience.
