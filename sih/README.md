# Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Research Pack

Compiled for Srikrishna O S — CSE Section A, SSN College of Engineering.

This folder is a research compilation for first-time SIH participants. It covers the official format, problem-statement selection, team building, judging criteria, past winning projects, SSN-specific examples, and practical tips from winners/evaluators.

This is **not** an official document. Always cross-check with [sih.gov.in](https://sih.gov.in) and your college SPOC for the latest rules.
