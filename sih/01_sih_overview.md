# SIH Overview, Eligibility and Rules

## What is SIH?

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is a nationwide open-innovation hackathon run by the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC) and AICTE. Government ministries, state departments, PSUs, private companies and NGOs post real-world problem statements; student teams compete to build the best software or hardware solution.

- Official site: https://sih.gov.in
- Started in 2017 as software-only; hardware edition began in 2018.
- Editions so far: 2017, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025. SIH 2020 was cancelled due to COVID-19.
- It is currently the 7th/8th edition depending on count.

## Two editions

| Edition | Focus | Typical output |
|---------|-------|----------------|
| Software | Web/mobile/AI/ML/cloud/cybersecurity/data solutions | Working prototype, demo, code repository |
| Hardware | IoT, embedded, robotics, electronics, devices | Physical working prototype/demonstration |

Multi-disciplinary teams are encouraged, especially for hardware. Software teams need strong programming skills across most members.

## Eligibility

- Undergraduate, postgraduate and PhD students of recognized Indian institutions.
- **All team members must be from the same college.** No inter-college teams.
- Members from different branches within the same college can form a team.
- Teams are selected through an **Internal Hackathon** conducted by the college.

## Team rules

- **Size:** 6 members, including a team leader.
- **Gender:** At least one female member is mandatory.
- **Same college:** All 6 must be from the same institute.
- **Team leader:** Usually the first member on the portal list.
- **Mentors:** Up to 2 mentors (faculty/industry) may be registered. Some colleges require at least one faculty mentor for the internal round.
- **Changes:** Team members can be changed before the finale, but the consent letter must match the final list.

## Prize money

| Edition | Typical prize per problem statement |
|---------|-------------------------------------|
| Software | INR 1,00,000 reported by winners |
| Hardware | INR 1,50,000 – 3,00,000 depending on the PS |

Prizes may also include internships, incubation support and national recognition.

## Important official constraints

- Students cannot register directly. The college SPOC handles registration and nomination.
- Only teams selected in the internal hackathon can be nominated.
- Colleges can nominate a limited number of teams (e.g., 30 shortlist + 5 waitlisted; some colleges use 45 + 5; confirm with your SPOC).
- Inter-college or inter-school teams are not allowed.
- For the finale, a printed college consent letter on letterhead with all members' names, team name, principal signature and college seal is usually required.
